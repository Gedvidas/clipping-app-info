# Clipping by Gedvidas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

## About this application

Clipping by Gedvidas is a desktop application that lets users select, review and send videos to TikTok for further editing and publishing.

The integration has been tested in TikTok Sandbox. Production review is pending, and public production access is not currently available.

## Information processed

When you authorize the application, it processes:

- Your TikTok account identifier, display name and profile picture returned through the user.info.basic permission.
- OAuth access and refresh tokens used to maintain your authorized connection.
- Video files you select and explicitly approve for upload.
- Upload identifiers, processing status and error information.

The application displays the connected account's name so you can check which account will receive your video.

Clipping does not request or store your TikTok password.

## How information is used

Account information identifies your connected TikTok account.

Tokens authorize API requests and refresh your connection when necessary.

Selected videos are sent to TikTok only after you review the video and confirm the upload. You finish editing and decide whether to publish within TikTok.

Upload identifiers and status information help confirm delivery and investigate failures.

## Storage and sharing

Access tokens, refresh tokens and upload results are stored in the operator's local workspace. Selected video files remain in their chosen local locations and are transmitted to TikTok when you authorize an upload.

Credentials, tokens and private media must not be committed to public repositories.

TikTok receives the selected videos and information necessary to perform the authorized actions. Its handling of this information is governed by its own privacy policy:

[TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en)

If you separately authorize an AI assistant to help operate or troubleshoot the application, information you provide to that assistant may be processed by its provider.

Personal information is not sold or used by Clipping for advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Local tokens and upload records are retained until they are removed by the operator or are no longer needed for the authorized workflow. Automatic deletion is not currently implemented.

You can revoke the application's access in your TikTok account settings. You can also contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion of locally stored personal information. These requests are handled manually.

Deleting local information does not delete videos or other information already held by TikTok. Manage that content through TikTok.
