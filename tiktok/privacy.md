# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

## Current status

Clipping's TikTok integration is under development.
It is not yet approved or available for production use.
This policy describes its intended data practices.

## Data and purpose

With your authorization, the integration will access your
TikTok account identifier, display name and profile picture
to identify the connected account.

It will send videos you select to TikTok drafts and process
upload identifiers and status information. You will review
and complete publication inside TikTok.

Clipping does not request your TikTok password.

## Storage and sharing

OAuth access and refresh tokens, working videos and upload
results are stored in the operator's local workspace.
They must not be committed to public repositories.

Selected videos and necessary account information are sent
to TikTok to perform the actions you authorize.

If you authorize an AI assistant to operate the workflow,
selected command output and metadata may be processed by
that assistant's provider.

Data is not sold or used for advertising.

## Retention and deletion

The operator retains working data only while needed for
the authorized workflow or applicable legal obligations.

Contact the operator below to request access, correction
or deletion of locally stored personal data. Requests are
handled manually; automatic cleanup is not currently provided.

You can revoke the integration's permissions in your TikTok
account settings. The operator must then stop using that
authorization and remove associated local tokens and data
that are no longer required.

Deleting local files does not delete content held by TikTok.
Manage that content within TikTok.

## Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect
visitor information, including IP addresses, as explained in
the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

TikTok processes data under its
[Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en).
