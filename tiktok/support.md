# Support and troubleshooting

Contact [gedvidas.lapienis@gmail.com](mailto:gedvidas.lapienis@gmail.com) for help with Clipping by Gedvidas.

Include the step you were performing, approximate time and error message. Remove credentials, tokens and private information from screenshots. Do not email your password, access token or client secret.

## I cannot connect my account

Production access has not been approved. A configured Sandbox permits its authorized test accounts; it is not public access. Ask the operator to confirm the testing configuration. If you declined authorization, no new access was granted.

## The wrong account is displayed

Do not confirm the transfer. Check which TikTok account was used during browser authorization and ask the operator to help reconnect the correct account.

## The video will not preview or upload

Confirm that the file exists and opens in your local player. Check the error message. A supported extension alone does not guarantee that the encoding or duration will be accepted. The desktop application inspects the video before transfer.

## Upload status is uncertain

Do not immediately send the same video again. The operator can check the existing upload identifier using the application's status command and local receipt. A timeout can happen after TikTok has received the upload. Share the error message privately with support so the existing attempt can be investigated.

## I do not see an Inbox notification

Check that the application confirmed delivery and your phone is signed into the same account. If delivery is pending or uncertain, check the existing upload status before retrying. Inbox delivery does not mean the video is already public.

## Can I publish or schedule from this website?

No. This is the product's documentation and support site. The desktop Inbox workflow sends a reviewed video to TikTok. Editing and publication are completed in TikTok.

## Remove access or request data deletion

Revoke Clipping's access in TikTok account settings. Email the operator to request deletion of locally retained information. Requests are handled manually. Manage content already sent to TikTok through TikTok. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html#retention-and-deletion).

## Current availability

As of 2 November 2026, Sandbox testing has succeeded, but the production submission was not approved. Website and branding corrections are being prepared for a future submission. Approval and a release date are not guaranteed.
