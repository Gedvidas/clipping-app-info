# Clipping by Gedvidas — Facebook Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 October 2026

## About the application

Clipping by Gedvidas is a local command-line tool for uploading approved videos to a Facebook Page.

The Meta application is currently unpublished. A read-only Page connection has been verified; a real upload has not yet been validated.

## Authorized use

Use the application only with Pages you own or are authorized to manage. Grant access only to the intended Pages.

Upload only content for which you have the necessary rights and permissions.

Your use must comply with applicable law and Meta's applicable terms and content policies.

## Review and confirmation

Before uploading, review the selected file, target Page, title, description and intended upload state.

DRAFT is the default state. Confirm an upload explicitly; public publishing requires explicit approval of the content and publishing action.

Processing status must be checked before treating an upload as complete.

If an operation has an uncertain result, check its status before retrying to avoid duplicates.

## Credentials and local media

Protect access to your computer, app credentials and authorization tokens.

Do not share credentials or tokens publicly or commit them to public repositories.

Maintain your own copies of original media.

Revoking app access does not automatically remove previously uploaded Facebook content.

## Availability

API access depends on Meta permissions, review or verification requirements, quotas and service availability.

Uploads may fail or take time to process. Uninterrupted service, successful processing and Meta approval are not guaranteed.
