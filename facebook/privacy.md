# Clipping by Gedvidas — Facebook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

## Operator and purpose

Operator: Gedvidas
Contact: gedvidas.lapienis@gmail.com

Clipping by Gedvidas is a local command-line application for managing approved video uploads to Facebook Pages.

The Meta application is currently unpublished. Page connectivity has been checked; a real video upload has not yet been validated.

## Information processed

Depending on the permissions you grant and the action you request, the application processes:

- Facebook Page identifiers, names, your available Page tasks and Page metadata.
- Authorization tokens used to access the selected Page.
- Local video files, titles and descriptions you choose for upload.
- Video identifiers, processing status and operational error information.

The current upload workflow does not download private messages or follower lists. Clipping does not request or store your Facebook password.

## Use of information

Page information identifies the intended destination. Tokens authorize API calls.

Videos and their titles and descriptions are sent to Meta only for an upload you authorize. Upload identifiers and status records help track processing and investigate failures.

The default upload state is DRAFT. Public publishing requires explicit confirmation.

Clipping does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

## Storage and recipients

Credentials and tokens are stored in private files on the operator's computer. Videos remain in their chosen local locations; upload records are stored in the local workspace.

Secrets and private media are excluded from public source repositories.

Meta receives the video and information necessary for the authorized API operation and handles them under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

If you separately authorize an AI assistant to help operate or troubleshoot the application, information you provide to it may be processed by its provider.

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Retention

Local credentials, tokens and operational records are retained until removed by the operator or no longer needed for the authorized workflow.

Automatic deletion is not currently implemented. Original media files are retained according to the operator's choices.

## Data deletion instructions

To request deletion of information held locally by Clipping:

1. Email **gedvidas.lapienis@gmail.com** with the subject **Facebook data deletion — Clipping**.
2. Provide the Facebook Page name or Page ID and describe the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, app secrets or access tokens.
3. The operator will verify the request as needed, manually remove the applicable local tokens and associated records, and reply when the request has been handled.

You may also request access to or correction of locally held personal information through the same contact.

You can revoke the app's access through your Facebook account's app or business integration settings.

Revoking access and deleting local information do not automatically delete videos or other information already held by Meta. Manage or delete that content through Facebook.

This page will be updated when the application's data practices change.
